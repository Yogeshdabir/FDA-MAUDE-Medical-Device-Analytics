# Limitations and References

## Data Interpretation

FDA MAUDE is a passive reporting system. Reports can be incomplete, inaccurate, duplicated, or submitted for different purposes. A report does not independently establish device causality.

## Rate Limitation

Report counts are not failure rates, incidence, or prevalence because exposure/utilization denominators are not provided by the MAUDE reporting dataset used here.

## Signal Interpretation

A reporting increase or unusual pattern is presented as a review candidate. It is not automatically labeled a safety signal or adverse trend.

## Synthetic Data

Investigation, root-cause, CAPA, and effectiveness information is synthetic demonstration data and must not be interpreted as FDA-sourced or manufacturer quality-system records.

## Source

Use the official FDA MAUDE source and record the exact download/snapshot date in each analytical run.
