# Executive Case Study

## Challenge

Public medical-device post-market reporting data is large, distributed, and subject to structural and completeness limitations.

## Approach

Built a controlled workflow spanning source assessment, business requirements, data-quality validation, relational modeling, SQL analytics, Power BI, testing/UAT, and a separate synthetic quality workflow.

## Key Design Decision

Separate report-level and device-level grains to prevent device-level multiplicity from inflating distinct report KPIs.

## Analytical Guardrail

MAUDE report counts are treated as reporting patterns. They are not presented as exposure-adjusted failure rates or proof of causality.

## Portfolio Value

Demonstrates MedTech/PMS domain understanding together with Business Analysis, Data Engineering, SQL, Power BI, Data Quality, and validation practices.
